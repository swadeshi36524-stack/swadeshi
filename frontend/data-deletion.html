Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how you can request the deletion of your personal information that we hold, and how we handle such requests.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Swadeshi Natural Products respects your right to control your personal information. You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you by following the instructions on this page. Requests are reviewed and actioned by our team; deletion is not automatic and is not immediate. Please see the details below.

1 What Personal Information This Covers

You may request deletion of the personal information we hold about you, which may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your delivery address and any delivery notes associated with your orders.
  • Your order history, including the products ordered and order details.
  • Your account information registered on our website.
  • Your WhatsApp interaction data, such as your WhatsApp phone number, your selected language and your conversation state with our automated assistant.

2 How to Submit a Deletion Request

To request deletion of your personal information, send us an email at the address below:

Email for deletion requests swadeshi36524@gmail.com

Please use the subject line "Data Deletion Request" so that your request is routed correctly. You may also contact us by phone or WhatsApp, but an email request ensures we have a written record of your instruction.

3 What to Include in Your Request

To help us locate your records and process your request accurately, please include:

  • Your full name.
  • The phone number and/or email address registered with us, so we can identify your account and records.
  • If you have placed orders, your order ID or order details if available.
  • The WhatsApp phone number you used to contact us, if you also want your WhatsApp interaction data deleted.
  • A clear statement that you are requesting deletion of your personal information.

Providing complete details helps us action your request faster and avoid any confusion with other customers' records.

4 Identity Verification May Be Required

To protect your account and prevent unauthorised deletion of data, we may need to verify your identity before processing your request. This may include confirming details you provided earlier, such as the phone number or email address registered with us.

  • If we cannot verify your identity, we may not be able to process the request.
  • We will only ask for the minimum information needed to confirm that the request is genuinely from you.

5 Retention Requirements

We will delete or anonymise the personal information you ask us to remove. However, we may need to retain certain records where we are required or permitted to do so by applicable law.

  • Order and payment records may be retained for a limited period to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • Some records may also be retained where necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or enforce our agreements.
  • Where retention is required, we will keep only the minimum information necessary for that purpose, and delete the rest.
Deletion is not automatic or immediate. Once identity is verified, we will action your request within a reasonable period, subject to the retention requirements described above.
